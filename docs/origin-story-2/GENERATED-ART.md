# Original scenery provenance

Built-in ImageGen was used, following the imagegen skill. No CLI/API fallback, external image search, Nintendo assets or protagonist generation was used.

Style reference: existing `assets/master-build/socal-scenes.png`. Generated source: `docs/origin-story-2/source/scenes.png`; original retained under the Codex generated-images folder as `exec-9d540ea3-1493-4429-a996-b974690d8891.png`.

Runtime asset: `assets/story2/scenes.webp`. Build: `scripts/build-story2.cjs` with optional build-time Sharp. The six-panel source is resized with nearest-neighbor to a 1024×864 atlas and encoded near-losslessly. Source 3,569,416 bytes → runtime 1,242,914 bytes; maximum channel deviation from the nearest-neighbor build reference: 2/255. Exact dimensions/hash are in `assets/story2/manifest.json`. Only the runtime WebP is precached, and the cinematic loads it lazily. Original PNG and review media are not runtime downloads. The existing SoCal art supplies the final scene.

## Generation prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Create ONE production background atlas for the seven-scene Origin Story 2.0 cinematic in 3Dudes1Quest. Input image is STYLE REFERENCE ONLY: match its rich original 16-bit pixel scenery, saturated sunset colors, crisp clustered pixels and platform-adventure identity. No humans or animals whatsoever: approved characters are composited later. Atlas layout: EXACTLY TWO columns and THREE rows of equally sized seamless rectangular panoramic panels, no gutters, borders, words, letters, logos or UI. Prefer 1536 by 1296 overall; six 768 by 432 panels. Each panel has a clear, quiet foreground promenade across its lowest quarter for three adult characters, leave central lower stage unobstructed. Top left: Dominican Republic Caribbean sunset, palms framing edges, turquoise ocean and warm peach-pink sky, intimate romantic tropical shore. Top right: the family's cozy shared home terrace, inviting amber windows, garden, subtle Pride accents on awning at edge; center foreground empty. Middle left: the same neighborhood during a blue-grey rainstorm, closed shop shutters and distant quiet street, symbolic economic/pandemic adversity, warm small window of hope, no scary figures and no text. Middle right: hopeful dawn breaking over a coastal hill and garden, soft rainbow-colored clouds HIGH in sky, quiet central stage; no rainbow ribbons near foreground. Bottom left: hopeful community garden beside restored river and green mountains, flowers and trees, colorful community neighborhood at edges; clear walking foreground. Bottom right: beautiful continuous five-destination vista in left-to-right order: Southern California palms/coast, Tahoe sapphire lake/pines, Alaska aurora/glacier, Hawaii volcanic tropical island, Costa Rica rainforest/beach with tiny welcoming retirement cottage at far right. All five readable as a journey but no dividers or labels. Consistent pixel density and palette across all panels, high-quality original SNES-era pixel environments. Avoid photorealism, 3D, blurry painting, generic smooth gradients, decorative rectangles, sparkle clutter, characters, faces or built-in narration. This is scenery only for an emotional 55-second retro game opening.
