# Alaska — The Aurora Return (3.4)

The master brief superseded the initial weather-machine encounter while preserving its implemented route, shelters, collectibles, relays and shared systems. The Frost Queen curses Alaska with blizzards and zombie salmon. Will, Daniel and Caleb follow Rigsby and Zoey from Tahoe to a remote trail station. Restore three Aurora Relays and break the Queen's curse. Her fictional signature line is “NO SARCASM IN THE LAST FRONTIER!”

## Route and mission

A 7,600-unit route crosses Trail Station, Frozen River, Glacier Pass and Aurora Ridge. Marked thin ice deals normal recoverable damage; upper stepping platforms provide safe crossings and optional rewards. Twelve shards, two supply caches and three healing shelter checkpoints reward exploration. Predictable snow gusts have advance warnings and stop at shelters. Relays require two power hits; all three must be restored before entering the arena.

## Enemies and companions

Cartoon zombie salmon patrol, warn, leap and flop. A river-platform school and ground patrols introduce their movement before the boss. Larger salmon appear in the final phase. Frost owls retain their warned pellets. No gore or real-person likenesses. Rigsby and Zoey use the redesigned beagle sprites and provide shelter dialogue. Their roles and the dudes' physics/powers remain unchanged.

## Frost Queen

An original pixel-drawn queen wears a winter cloak, ice crown and staff. Twelve health points establish phases at 12/8/4. Phase one uses snowballs and salmon. Phase two creates warned blizzards, icicles and temporary marked ice. Phase three combines attacks, larger salmon and a three-hit ice shield. Every character can break the shield. Warnings last 800 ms; icicle positions are fixed when warning begins. Active enemies are capped at twelve; ice patches at three; effects at one hundred. Death clears arena hazards/summons, resets the fight and returns to the final shelter with relay/treasure progress preserved.

Victory removes hazards, ends blizzards, converts the salmon back to harmless normal fish and restores a rainbow aurora. The ending marks Alaska complete and announces Costa Rica: Coming Soon. No fourth adventure exists.

QuestAdventure composes QuestRuntime's fixed-step loop, movement/camera/input and backup saves; QuestCore's cast/powers; QuestCast's world outfits; QuestRetro's renderer; QuestAudio's single graph; QuestViewport's pause/save/input-release/resume contract; QuestJourney's badges. Alaska uses a separate versioned save key. SoCal and Tahoe retain their established runtime files and save keys. Pausing, leaving the page or rotating saves the checkpoint and releases held controls. Resume remains explicit.
