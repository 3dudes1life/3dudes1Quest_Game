# Approval document: future encounter structure

This is an impact assessment, **not an implemented redesign**. The owner's audit brief says: “Do not silently move boss encounters or rewrite progression before documenting the impact on existing saves and obtaining approval for any substantial redesign.”

## SoCal: mid-level Queen → final Court1/3/9

Current six-card/three-beacon/captain gate admits the Court at7980. Queen begins after Court and directly triggers Zoey's rescue, the passport completion and Tahoe portal. Both fights occupy City Hall. The final card is at7580; simply moving Queen earlier would put her behind prerequisites that cannot yet be met. An owner-approved plan must select her location, local prerequisites, checkpoint and introduction; retain the six cards/beacons; and reserve City Hall for the final Court.

Nine judges at the current90-unit spacing reach x8870 with a46-wide body, overlapping the current x8840 portal region. Although shots can reach them, encounter boundaries, visibility, projectile density, attack staggering and escape space need testing. Changing5 to9 alone is not a complete redesign.

Proposed migration policy for review:

1. Preserve old completed adventures, Zoey, cards, beacons, score and Tahoe access; offer an explicit replay of new final content.
2. Preserve an in-progress Queen's HP/phase; resume at a documented safe checkpoint. Do not silently rewind collectibles or label the newly final Court defeated merely because Queen was active.
3. Preserve a recorded Court victory as legacy encounter credit, with an optional updated nine-judge replay; distinguish genuine victory from grandfathered flags.
4. Introduce explicit encounter-stage metadata, migrate old fields without deleting backups, and gate rescue/Tahoe on final-stage completion only for fresh adventures under the new structure.
5. Test death, save, rotation, reload and offline at every stage, including mid-level Queen defeat, interrupted rescue, both legacy boss states and already completed saves.

Approval needed: location/prerequisite map, replay/completion policy, checkpoint/arena changes and nine-judge difficulty budget. No such stage or arena changes are included in this PR.

## Tahoe: Toxic Bloom → Pine Beetle Queen

Approve a lake-side encounter boundary, contamination state, algae/bubble hazard language, checkpoint and restoration event. Preserve memories, four nests, existing weather, Forest Drain and summit portal. Existing Queen/completed saves must retain access and forest progress; introduce optional Bloom replay rather than forcing a completed player to restart. Define whether Bloom gates the climbing trail or only the lake's restoration. No Toxic Bloom entity or production sprite pack exists in the current runtime.

## Alaska: Pipeline Meltdown → Frost Queen

Approve the leak/valve sequence, worker/drone behavior, glacier change, flood escape timing, safe checkpoint and restoration state. Keep existing relays, shelters, shards/caches, weather and Frost Queen unless the owner explicitly approves integration changes. Wildlife must not become replacement combat enemies; wolves and polar bears currently do not appear as enemies. Clarify the existing hostile owl role. Legacy completed saves should retain Costa Rica Coming Soon completion, with optional encounter replay. A flood cannot be represented truthfully by renaming the present thin-ice hazard.

Costa Rica and Hawaii remain future destinations. No new adventure is proposed or implemented here.
